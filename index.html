About Project

About Lagos Life

Lagos Life is a community-driven ecosystem for people connected to Lagos — its culture, commerce and creators. The project is building products and community spaces intended to grow into a wider token-enabled ecosystem.

About $LGL

$LGL is the planned token for the wider Lagos Life ecosystem, intended to provide token-based utility within that ecosystem when officially launched and enabled. It has not launched. The current target launch date is March 1, 2027, and is subject to change through official announcements.

About $LGL Miner

$LGL Miner is an interface for tracking participation — it is not a blockchain miner and it does not perform proof-of-work.

Lagos Life Integration

$LGL is intended to be integrated into the wider Lagos Life game and ecosystem, providing token-based utility there once it is officially launched and enabled.

The Lagos Life game has its own internal virtual currency. That currency is separate from LGL Points and from $LGL, and no relationship between them has been announced. LGL Points do not convert into the Lagos Life virtual currency, and this application does not display any such conversion.

How Mining Points Work

  • Taps — each tap earns points and consumes energy.
  • Energy — refills continuously; the tank refills fully every hour.
  • Tasks — eligible actions award one-time or daily bonuses.
  • Bonuses — daily claims and rewarded-ad rewards.
  • Referrals — verified invites may credit referral points.
  • Possible future boosts — temporary multipliers from tasks, events or rewarded ads.

How the Airdrop Works

  1. Users accumulate LGL Points.
  2. Eligible activity is recorded.
  3. An official snapshot is taken.
  4. Eligibility is verified.
  5. Final allocations are calculated.
  6. Claim functionality opens according to official instructions.
Points vs $LGL. LGL Points are not $LGL tokens. Points currently have no exchange or withdrawal function. Your final $LGL allocation will be determined according to the official airdrop rules and snapshot.

$LGL Conversion

Not available yet. Keep earning LGL Points. The official $LGL allocation and conversion formula will be announced during the appropriate airdrop phase.

There is no LGL Points → $LGL exchange rate during the Mining Phase, and no estimate is calculated or shown anywhere in this application.

Airdrop Eligibility

Final eligibility is determined by official rules. Completing any action in this application does not by itself guarantee an allocation. Activity is subject to anti-cheat and anti-bot review before the snapshot.

Privacy

Prototype behaviour. This build stores your progress only in your own browser. Nothing is transmitted to a server.

What is stored

  • Your points, energy, tap count and multiplier.
  • Daily bonus state and completed tasks.
  • Your referral code and locally recorded referral state.
  • Your in-app preferences (haptics, notifications).

In this browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data, or using the reset option in Profile, permanently erases it.
